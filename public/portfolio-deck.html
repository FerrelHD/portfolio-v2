TACTIQ

ROLE: Front-End Lead & UI Engineer
SYSTEM: Next.js 14 · TypeScript · HTML5 Canvas · Socket.io · Chart.js · Tailwind
CONTEXT: Telemetry Engineering & Football Analytics Platform

Modern football coaching staff and analysts face an information overload: telemetry data streams, raw tracking coordinates, and delayed video recordings exist in fragmented silos. Analysts spent hours reconciling coordinate feeds without real-time spatial vectors.

As Front-End Lead, I architected the core visualization pipeline in Next.js 14. We isolated high-frequency coordinate broadcasts from React DOM reconciliations by utilizing a double-buffered HTML5 Canvas 2D engine driven by requestAnimationFrame. Synchronized linear interpolation prevents packet jitter, rendering live pitch heatmaps and passing corridors smoothly.
